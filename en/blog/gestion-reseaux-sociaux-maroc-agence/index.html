Social media management in Morocco: should you entrust it to an agency?

Discover the advantages of entrusting your social media management to a professional agency in Morocco and the strategies that work in 2026.

  1. Home
  2. /
  3. Blog
  4. /
  5. Social media management in Morocco: should you entrust it to an agency?

Les social networks in Morocco have become the primary communication channel between businesses and their clients. With over 22 million active users on Facebook, 8 million on Instagram, and explosive growth of TikTok, ignoring these platforms in 2026 means turning a blind eye to a massive pool of prospects. But the question asked by many Moroccan entrepreneurs is legitimate: should you manage your networks in-house or hire a social media management agency in Morocco ?

The state of social networks in Morocco in 2026

The Moroccan digital landscape has evolved significantly in recent years. Morocco now has more than 35 million internet users, and social networks represent the main online activity of Moroccans. Facebook remains the dominant social network with impressive penetration across all age groups. Instagram is experiencing strong growth, particularly among 18-35 year olds, and is establishing itself as the preferred channel for the lifestyle, fashion, catering, and tourism sectors.

TikTok is the true revolution of recent years in Morocco. The platform has won over millions of Moroccan users and offers advertising opportunities still under-exploited by local businesses. LinkedIn is asserting itself as the essential network for B2B and recruitment in Morocco, with a constantly expanding community of professionals.

In-house vs. agency management: pros and cons

Managing in-house: when is it relevant?

In-house management can work for very small structures where the manager is themselves a public figure (influencer, chef, artist). Authenticity and spontaneity are then assets. However, the professional social media management requires a considerable time investment: visual content creation, post writing, replying to comments and messages, statistical analysis, competitive intelligence. On average, serious management mobilizes 15 to 25 hours per week — a time that most entrepreneurs cannot afford.

Entrusting to an agency: the decisive advantages

Calling upon a social media agency in Morocco offers several major strategic advantages. Firstly, you benefit from a team of specialists : community managers, graphic designers, photographers, and digital strategists work together to produce high-quality content adapted to each platform. Secondly, an agency has the professional tools planning, analysis, and monitoring tools whose individual cost would be prohibitive for an SME.

Thirdly, an agency ensures brand consistency sur l'ensemble de vos canaux : même ton éditorial, même visual identity, même calendrier de publication régulier. Cette régularité est essentielle pour fidéliser votre communauté et développer votre notoriété.

How much does social media management cost in Morocco?

The rates of an community management agency in Morocco vary depending on the scope of services and the number of platforms managed. Here are the price ranges practiced in 2026:

Essential Plan (2 social networks) : between 1,500 to 3,000 MAD/month. Includes 8 to 12 posts per month, visual creation, comment moderation, and a monthly performance report.

Premium Plan (3-4 social networks) : between 3,000 to 6,000 MAD/month. Includes 15 to 25 posts per month, stories, Instagram Reels and TikTok videos, a comprehensive editorial strategy, management of organic advertising campaigns, and detailed analytical tracking.

Custom Plan (full service) : starting from 6,000 MAD/month. For ambitious brands requiring intensive presence: daily content, professional photo and video shoots, influencer marketing, crisis management, and advanced reporting.

The strategies that work in Morocco in 2026

Short video content : Instagram Reels and TikTok videos generate 3 to 5 times more engagement than static posts. «Behind-the-scenes» formats, tutorials, and customer testimonials perform particularly well with the Moroccan audience.

Local influencer marketing : Collaborating with Moroccan micro-influencers (5,000 to 50,000 followers) offers an excellent return on investment. Their audience is engaged, local, and trusts their recommendations.

Content in Darija : Posts in Moroccan dialect consistently generate more engagement than those in French or classical Arabic. A good agency knows how to adapt the tone and linguistic register to each platform and target audience.

Conclusion

La social media management in Morocco est un levier de croissance trop important pour être traité de manière amateur. Confier cette mission à une agence spécialisée vous permet de vous concentrer sur votre cœur de métier tout en bénéficiant d'une stratégie digitale professionnelle et mesurable.
