Technological Context

Why AI Sovereignty has become a vital strategic imperative in Morocco

Sovereign AI in Morocco refers to deploying cutting-edge open-source artificial intelligence models — such as Llama 3 (Meta) and DeepSeek — directly on servers physically located in Morocco or within your own headquarters (On-Premise).

This approach strictly satisfies Law 09-08 on personal data protection and aligns with all guidelines issued by the CNDP (National Commission for Personal Data Protection).

At Mahdi Créations, we engineer this technological transition. Unlike proprietary cloud APIs (OpenAI ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Microsoft Copilot), sovereign AI ensures that no commercial, legal, or sensitive data ever leaves Moroccan territory. In alignment with the Morocco Digital 2030 roadmap and the ADD (Digital Development Agency), we turn digital sovereignty into a lasting competitive advantage.

Architecture & Operations

How Does Sovereign AI Work?

Our engineering approach at Mahdi Créations is built on a private, air-tight architecture designed to seamlessly interface with your existing IT workflow:

01

Local Hosting (On-Premise / National Cloud)

We install powerful open-source AI models directly on your corporate servers or certified Moroccan cloud infrastructure. The AI operates fully offline without external Internet reliance (Air-Gapped).

02

Private Search Engine (Private RAG)

We index your enterprise document repositories (PDFs, Excel spreadsheets, internal databases) locally. Your teams can securely query records and retrieve synthesized, accurate answers in seconds.

03

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

The system authenticates the user's role before retrieving data. The model strictly respects authorization boundaries (e.g., non-authorized staff can never query payroll, HR, or board materials).

04

"Read-Only" Legacy Integration

The AI interacts with your current systems (ERP, CRM, DMS) in read-only mode without modifying core databases. Your operational records remain pristine with zero data corruption risk.
