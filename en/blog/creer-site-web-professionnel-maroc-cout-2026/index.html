Creating a professional website in Morocco: how much does it cost in 2026?

La creation of a professional website in Morocco has become an essential investment for any company wishing to develop in 2026. Whether you are an artisan in Marrakech, a restaurateur in Casablanca, or a real estate developer in Tangier, your online presence directly determines your ability to attract new customers. But how much should you really plan for a quality website? We offer you a complete and transparent guide to prices on the Moroccan market.

The different types of websites and their rates in Morocco

The cost of creation of a website in Morocco varies considerably depending on the complexity of the project, required features, and expected quality level. Here is a detailed overview of price ranges for each type of project in 2026:

The showcase site (one-page or multi-page)

Un professional showcase site est la solution idéale pour présenter votre activité, your services et vos coordonnées. Il s'agit du type de site le plus demandé au Maroc, notamment par les PME, les professions libérales et les commerçants. Le prix d'un site vitrine de qualité au Maroc se situe généralement entre 3,000 MAD to 10,000 MAD. This price includes a custom responsive design (mobile-friendly), integration of 4 to 8 pages, a contact form, basic SEO optimization, and hosting for the first year. For a high-end showcase site with advanced animations, premium design, and an elaborate content strategy, rates can reach 15,000 to 25,000 MAD.

The e-commerce site

La creation of an online store in Morocco implies a more substantial investment, justified by technical complexity and necessary integrations. A professional e-commerce site costs between 7,000 MAD to 20,000 MAD depending on the number of products, payment gateways (CMI, PayPal, bank transfer), and desired features (inventory management, shipping, promotions). The most used platforms in Morocco are WooCommerce (WordPress), PrestaShop and custom-developed solutions in Next.js ou React. The integration of CMI (Centre Monétique Interbancaire) online payment represents an additional cost of 1,000 to 2,500 MAD depending on the complexity.

The custom web application

For companies with specific needs (internal CRM, booking platform, analytical dashboard, SaaS), the development of a custom web application is necessary. Rates start from 15,000 MAD and can exceed 60,000 MAD for complex projects. These applications are generally developed with modern technologies like Next.js, Node.js, React and deployed on secure cloud infrastructures.

Factors that influence the price of your website

Several elements determine the final cost of your project of website creation in Marrakech or elsewhere in Morocco:

Design and user experience (UX/UI) : A premium design, custom-made by an experienced art director, represents a higher investment than a customized template. The visual quality of your site directly reflects your company's image. At Mahdi Créations, every mockup is designed to maximize visitor engagement and conversion rate.

The number of pages and features : The more pages, complex forms, integrations (Google Maps, booking system, live chat, multi-language), and advanced features your site has, the more costly the development will be. It is essential to clearly define your specifications before requesting a quote.

SEO optimization : A website without search engine optimization is an invisible site. Technical SEO optimization (loading speed, markup, code structure), keyword research, and optimized content writing represent an additional investment of 2,000 to 5,000 MAD which quickly pays for itself thanks to the generated organic traffic.

Hosting and maintenance : Secure web hosting in Morocco costs between 500 to 1,500 MAD per year depending on the required performance. Technical maintenance (security updates, backups, bug fixes) is billed between 1,500 to 3,000 MAD per year as a subscription plan.

How to choose the right web agency in Morocco?

The choice of your web agency in Marrakech or in Morocco is decisive for the success of your project. Here are our recommendations:

Examine the portfolio : A serious agency has a varied portfolio of recent work. Check the quality of the sites created, their loading speed, and mobile adaptation. At Mahdi Créations, we proudly present over 200 successful projects.

Check technical skills : Make sure the agency masters modern technologies (Next.js, React, Node.js) and not just basic CMS. Your site's technical performance directly impacts your Google SEO.

Request a detailed quote : A professional quote should detail every expense item: design, development, content, SEO, hosting, and maintenance. Beware of abnormally low prices that often hide compromises on quality or hidden fees.
