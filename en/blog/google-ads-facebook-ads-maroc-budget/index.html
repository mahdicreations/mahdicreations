Google Ads and Facebook Ads in Morocco: what budget to get results?

What budget to plan for effective Google Ads and Facebook Ads campaigns in Morocco in 2026? Discover the real costs and winning strategies.

Digital advertising in Morocco is a powerful growth lever when strategically managed. Too many businesses launch ad campaigns without clear tracking, defined conversion funnels, or ongoing audience optimization, needlessly burning ad spend. In 2026, the Moroccan online advertising landscape demands a disciplined, data-driven approach.

Google Ads in Morocco: how it works and costs

Google Ads allows you to display your business at the top of Google search results when your potential customers search for your products or services.

Cost per click (CPC) in Morocco

In Morocco, the average cost per click on Google Ads varies considerably depending on the sector: Real Estate : 1 to 3 MAD per click. Tourism and hospitality : 0.5 to 2 MAD. E-commerce : 0.3 to 1.5 MAD. Legal services : 2 to 5 MAD.

Recommended budget for Google Ads in Morocco

Testing phase (1-2 months) : between 1,000 to 2,500 MAD/month. Optimization phase (3-6 months) : between 2,500 to 7,000 MAD/month. Growth phase (6+ months) : 7,000 MAD/month and up.

Facebook Ads and Instagram Ads in Morocco

Facebook and Instagram ads in Morocco offer exceptionally precise targeting parameters: age, geographical location, interests, purchasing behaviors.

Recommended budget for Facebook Ads in Morocco

Small local business : 800 to 2,000 MAD/month. SME with regional ambition : 2,500 to 7,000 MAD/month. E-commerce brand : 5,000 to 20,000 MAD/month.

Budgetary errors to avoid

Spending without strategy : Boosting a Facebook post is not an advertising strategy. Stopping too early : Google and Facebook algorithms require data to optimize.

How to maximize your advertising ROI in Morocco?

Test and iterate : Systematically create 3 to 5 ad variations. Optimize your landing pages : The best advertising targeting is useless if your landing page doesn't convert. Use remarketing : Retarget your website visitors who did not convert.
