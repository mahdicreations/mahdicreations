Logo, brand guidelines, branding: why your brand image matters in Morocco

Why branding and visual identity are essential for your business in Morocco? Complete guide from logo creation to brand guidelines.

  2. /

In an increasingly competitive Moroccan market, thevisual identity de votre entreprise is often the first — and sometimes the only — contact a potential client will have with your brand before making a purchasing decision. In 2026, the branding in Morocco has become a major strategic lever.

What exactly is branding?

Le branding (or branding strategy) goes far beyond a simple logo. It encompasses all elements that define the public perception of your company: your visual identity (logo, colors, typography), your tone of communication, your values and your positioning on the market.

The essential components of visual identity

The logo: your visual signature

Le logo is the cornerstone of your brand identity. It must be memorable, unique, versatile (work large and small, in color and black and white) and reflect the values of your company.

The color palette

Les colors of your brand communiquent des émotions et des valeurs avant même que le client ne lise un seul mot. The choice of your palette chromatique doit être stratégique : gold and black → luxury and prestige. Blue → trust and professionalism. Green → nature and wellness.

Typography

The choice of fonts considerably influences the perception of your brand. Serif typography communicates elegance. Sans-serif font conveys modernity.

Brand guidelines: the guide to your identity

La brand guidelines is the reference document that defines the rules for using all visual elements of your brand. It guarantees the consistency of your communication.

How much does branding cost in Morocco?

Logo creation only : between 1,500 to 5,000 MAD. Complete visual identity (logo + brand guidelines + variations): between 4,000 to 12,000 MAD. Complete branding (strategy + identity + materials): between 10,000 to 25,000 MAD.

Conclusion

Le branding and visual identity in Morocco are strategic investments that directly impact the perception of your company, the trust of your clients, and your revenue.
